# Roadmap

## Slice A — minimum organism

- Persist energy, escrow, causal edges, and fitness as append-only events.
- Connect existing AMOS/Harbor receipts to the host gate. The initial JSONL
  research adapter is a verified hash chain; production moves the same ordering
  contract to DynamoDB.
- Settle every cloud Harbor iteration into a per-run organism ledger before its
  artifacts leave the runner; preserve positive and negative experience.
- Vest only consumed contributions cited by the official verifier.
- Decay unused provisional credit and claw back regressions.
- Add interruptible leases so steering, stopping, and sleep recovery are safe.

Exit: busy work cannot purchase cross-mission survival.

## Slice B — procedural heredity

- Expand the first admitted AMOS-owned seed genes (bounded provider recovery,
  typed-tool recovery, and lossless context compaction) with
  inspect-then-compile, deliver-first, repair-only-cited-gaps, and
  stop-on-hot-flat-lease.
- Reuse genes on unseen missions.
- Add controlled mutation and recombination with immutable lineage.
- Keep fixed specialist roles while procedure attribution stabilizes.

Exit: a procedure vests on one mission and generalizes to another without a
weight change.

## Slice C — contextual ecology

- Key existing pheromones by phase, artifact class, failure mode, and tool
  family.
- Preserve typed signals; remove the global signed-signal collapse.
- Maintain a small novelty archive without paying novelty rewards.

Exit: local evidence changes local behavior without global superstition or
population collapse.

## Slice D — deliberation control

- Train value-of-computation only on vested quality deltas.
- Add challenge/stop/reallocate decisions at safe lease boundaries.
- Treat recovery as an outcome and efficiency as a tie-breaker.

Exit: long reasoning remains possible when quality warrants it, while stagnant
leases become interruptible.

## Slice E — world learning and substrate training

- Keep exact state authoritative and HRR state associative.
- Benchmark the existing typed tabular transition model.
- Train an HRR transition head as a separate treatment.
- Fine-tune Qwen adapters on rights-eligible AMOS and public traces.
- Compare base Qwen, trained Qwen, fixed swarm, and evolving organism on frozen
  holdouts and external benchmarks.

Exit: model-weight gains and organism-level gains are independently measurable.

## Slice F — production intelligence

- Serve the organism behind the AMOS Hosted intelligence contract.
- Scale logical workers over shared model weights and continuous batching.
- Evolve team topology only after procedural credit is stable.
- Add multimodal encoders without weakening receipt or authority boundaries.

Exit: the organism is a governed, replaceable intelligence substrate—not an
experiment-specific harness.
